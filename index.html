Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Can't I just ask ChatGPT?

You can ask it for general tips, and they'll often be sensible. It can't compare every competitor and review in your area this week, and when it lacks data it guesses. Try it, then compare with our free preview.

How accurate is the data?

Prices, amenities and reviews are counted from what's listed on the booking sites each week. Booking rates are estimates, clearly labelled, with a High, Medium or Low confidence level and a "Data as of" date on every result. An area with too little data is never called Best.

Does it work for hotels, or only Airbnb?

Every kind of stay: hotels, resorts, homestays, villas, serviced apartments, guest houses, hostels, farm stays and camps. You're only ever compared with the same type, in the same area, at a similar size.

Which cities do you cover?

We're starting with Goa, then Udaipur, Rishikesh and Manali, adding cities every month. You can request a new city as an add-on.

Do you change my prices automatically?

Not yet. We suggest normal, weekend and festival prices, and you stay in control. Automatic syncing is on our roadmap.

My family shares one Wi-Fi. Can we all sign up?

Yes. Each person signs up with their own Google account and phone number. Paid plans also include team seats, so you can share one account properly.

What if there isn't enough data for my area?

You'll see a Low confidence label before you pay. If you've bought a report and our data turns out to be too thin, you get a full refund.
